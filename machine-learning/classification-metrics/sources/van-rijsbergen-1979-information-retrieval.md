# van Rijsbergen, *Information Retrieval*, 2nd ed. (Butterworths, 1979), Chapter 7 "Evaluation"

Supports: the retrieved/relevant contingency table (retrieved and not retrieved on rows, relevant and non-relevant on columns) with precision $\lvert A\cap B\rvert/\lvert B\rvert$ and recall $\lvert A\cap B\rvert/\lvert A\rvert$; the effectiveness measure $E = 1 - 1/(\alpha(1/P) + (1-\alpha)(1/R))$ with $\alpha = 1/(\beta^2+1)$, for a user who "attaches $\beta$ times as much importance to recall as precision"; at $\alpha = 1/2$ ($\beta = 1$) $E$ is a normalised symmetric difference of the relevant and retrieved sets.
