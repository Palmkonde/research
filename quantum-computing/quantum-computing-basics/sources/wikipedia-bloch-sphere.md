# Wikipedia: Bloch sphere

Encyclopedia article on the Bloch sphere: pure qubit states as the complex projective line, mixed states inside the Bloch ball, the expansion $\rho = \tfrac12(I + \vec a\cdot\vec\sigma)$, and the remark that generalizations to $n$-level systems are less useful to visualize.
