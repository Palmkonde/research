# Li, Ding and Xie, "Tackling the Qubit Mapping Problem for NISQ-Era Quantum Devices" (ASPLOS 2019)

The SABRE paper (arXiv:1809.02573v2): Section IV-A builds a DAG of two-qubit-gate dependencies, leaving out single-qubit gates, and defines the front layer F as the two-qubit gates with no unexecuted predecessors; Algorithm 1 executes gates from F and adds successors whose dependencies are resolved.
