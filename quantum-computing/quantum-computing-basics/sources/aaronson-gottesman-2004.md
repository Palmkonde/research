# Aaronson & Gottesman 2004: Improved Simulation of Stabilizer Circuits

Scott Aaronson and Daniel Gottesman, Phys. Rev. A 70, 052328 (2004), arXiv:quant-ph/0406196: states the Gottesman-Knill theorem, introduces the stabilizer formalism (a state represented by the group of unitaries, and in particular Pauli operators, that leave it unchanged), and gives the CHP tableau simulator.
