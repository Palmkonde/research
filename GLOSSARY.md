# Glossary

**Field**: A broad area of knowledge that groups related Topics (e.g. low-level, quantum computing, fundamentals).

**Topic**: One area of curiosity inside a Field (e.g. "CPU caches", "qubits"). Groups Questions.

**Question**: One concrete thing to be answered inside a Topic. The atomic unit of research.

**Finding**: The written answer to one Question, citing its Sources.

**Source**: An external reference a Finding relies on (paper, article, docs, book), recorded as its own note with a short description.

**Prerequisite**: A Topic that must be understood before another Topic. Lives in its own home Field; the dependent Topic links to it, never copies it.

**Deep dive**: Optional extended part of a Finding, added on request, beyond the short TL;DR and explanation.

**Project**: A time-boxed piece of work with its own goal, plan, deadline and deliverable (e.g. a literature review for a supervisor, a paper reproduction). Draws on Topics through links but does not own them.
